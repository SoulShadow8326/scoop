Structured Verification Engines

Scoop runs four specialized verification nodes to analyze claims, rejecting authority declarations in favor of evidence strength calculations.

Trust Passports

Maintains long-term reliability and metadata profiles for news sources, publishers, and community accounts based on evidence density.

Citation Graphs

Traces narrative origin and propagation pathways to identify if a rumor is independently verified or simply duplicated in an echo chamber.

Context Integrity

Scans metadata and layouts to flag cropped screenshots, missing timestamps, platform mismatches, and selectively edited quotes.

Emotional Manipulation

Measures the intensity of fear, anger, and outrage framing in text designed to bypass logical reasoning and provoke community panic.

Information DNA

Generates semantic fingerprints for claims, linking reposts, paraphrases, screenshots, and translated variants into a single traceable narrative family.

Courtroom Reasoning

Uses adversarial AI agents acting as prosecutor, defense, cross-examiner, and judge to challenge assumptions before forming a confidence-rated conclusion.
